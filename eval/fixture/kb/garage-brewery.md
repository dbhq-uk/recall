# Garage Brewery Log

Notes on the all-grain setup in the garage, recipes, and a running log of batches. Started brewing extract kits in 2021, moved to all-grain in spring 2022 after the Brewzilla arrived.

## Kit

The core kit is a Brewzilla Gen 4 35L all-in-one electric system. It replaced a three-vessel gravity setup that took up the whole bench and, frankly, half of two Saturdays a month. Grain gets milled on a Brewferm two-roller mill clamped to the edge of the chest freezer, which doubles as the fermentation chamber.

### Fermentation temperature control

The chest freezer runs an Inkbird ITC-308 controller wired inline. Probe taped to the side of the fermenter with a bit of pipe lagging over it for insulation from ambient air. Typical set points:

- Ales: 18-19°C
- Lagers (rare, patience isn't my strong suit): 11-12°C
- Diacetyl rest: raise 2°C over final 48 hours

### Water treatment

Tap water here is moderately hard, decent for bitters but needs adjusting for pale, hoppy styles. I use Bru'n Water spreadsheet to calculate additions. For a typical 23L pale ale batch:

```text
# water salt additions - Citra Pale, 23L batch
gypsum (CaSO4)      6.5 g
calcium chloride    4.0 g
epsom salt (MgSO4)  1.0 g
# target profile: Ca 150, SO4 200, Cl 60
```

## Cleaning

PBW soak overnight, then Star San no-rinse sanitiser before every transfer. Non-negotiable — the one infected batch (a saison, 2022) taught that lesson the hard way.

## Yeast strains used

A running list of what's gone into the fermenter and how it performed:

- **Safale US-05** — workhorse dry yeast, clean, ferments 18-22°C, used for six batches so far, never a bad result.
- **Wyeast 1056 American Ale** — liquid smack-pack, needs a starter for anything over 1.060 OG. Slightly fruitier than US-05 at the same temperature.
- **Mangrove Jack's M44 West Coast IPA** — good attenuation, dry finish, this is now the default for hoppy pales.
- **Lallemand Verdant IPA** — used once for a NEIPA attempt, gives that hazy, stone-fruit character people rave about online. Underperformed on attenuation though, finished at 1.016 when I expected 1.010.

## Batch log

**Batch 14 — Citra Pale Ale, brewed 3 March 2024.** Grain bill: 4.2kg Maris Otter, 0.3kg Caramalt, 0.15kg Carapils. Mashed at 66°C for 60 minutes, single infusion. Sparged to collect 27L pre-boil, boiled 60 minutes down to 23L into the fermenter.

Hop schedule was the whole point of this brew — wanted to see what Citra alone could do at three different additions:

```text
# hop schedule - Batch 14, Citra Pale
60 min    Citra 15g       (bittering, ~35 IBU contribution)
15 min    Citra 20g       (flavour)
0 min     Citra 25g       (whirlpool, 80°C, 20 min stand)
dry hop   Citra 40g       (day 4 of fermentation, 3 days)
```

OG came in at 1.052, pitched Mangrove Jack's M44 rehydrated per packet instructions, fermented at 19°C in the chest freezer. Airlock activity started within 11 hours, which is fast even for a healthy pitch — probably slightly over-pitched given how much yeast was left in the packet.

FG landed at 1.010 after nine days, giving 5.5% ABV. Dry hopped on day four as planned, then cold crashed for 48 hours at 2°C before kegging. This is genuinely one of the best batches to date — resinous, grapefruit-forward, good head retention thanks to the small Carapils addition. Kegged into a 19L Cornelius keg, force carbonated at 30psi for 36 hours with the keg rocked on its side, then dropped serving pressure to 12psi.

Tasting notes at two weeks: the Citra dry hop had mellowed slightly, more melon and less cat-pee-sharp than at kegging time (a known Citra trait when young). Would repeat this recipe exactly. One change for next time: consider a small Amarillo addition at whirlpool to round out the citrus.

**Batch 15 — Export Stout, brewed 14 April 2024.** A change of pace after three consecutive pale ales. Grain bill: 4.5kg Maris Otter, 0.4kg Chocolate Malt, 0.3kg Roasted Barley, 0.25kg Flaked Oats. Mash at 67°C, 60 minutes. OG 1.061.

Pitched Safale US-05 (no liquid yeast on hand, and honestly US-05 does fine for stouts too). Fermented at 19°C, 12 days, FG 1.014, 6.2% ABV. No dry hops — 20g Fuggles at 15 minutes was the only hop addition, purely for a touch of earthy bitterness in the background. Bottled this one rather than kegging (12 x 660ml swing tops plus the rest into standard 500ml bottles) with 6g/L of dextrose for priming.
