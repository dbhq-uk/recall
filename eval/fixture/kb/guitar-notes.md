# Guitar Notes

Practice log, gear notes, and songs learned. Started again properly in late 2022 after about a decade of barely touching it.

## Gear

### Electric

Fender Player Telecaster (Mexican-made), Butterscotch Blonde finish, bought secondhand in 2019 from a shop in Sheffield for £450. Maple neck, alder body, single-coil pickups both positions. Action set at 1.6mm at the 12th fret on the low E, 1.4mm on the high E — measured with feeler gauges after the neck needed a slight relief adjustment in winter 2023 (truss rod, quarter turn, cold weather always tightens things up).

#### Amplifier

Amp is a Fender Blues Junior IV, 15W tube combo, single 12" Celestion speaker. Runs it clean most of the time and lets pedals do the dirty work, though the built-in drive channel is decent for light breakup.

### Acoustic

Yamaha FG800, dreadnought, solid spruce top. Bought new, it's the "beater" that lives by the sofa for evenings when plugging in feels like too much effort.

### Pedalboard

Signal chain, tuner first:

```text
# pedalboard signal chain
guitar -> Boss TU-3 tuner -> Boss DS-1 distortion
       -> TC Electronic Hall of Fame 2 reverb -> amp input
```

The DS-1 is set fairly conservatively — most of its character comes through at low gain settings, cranking it just turns it to fuzz which isn't really the sound I'm after. Hall of Fame 2 lives mostly on the "spring" setting for that clean Telecaster twang.

### Strings

Ernie Ball Regular Slinky, 10-46 gauge, on the Telecaster. Changed roughly every six weeks, or sooner if a string starts sounding dull. The FG800 has D'Addario EJ16 phosphor bronze lights, changed far less often — maybe three times a year given how little it gets played hard.

## Practice routine

### Warm-up

Chromatic exercise across all six strings, four notes per string, starting at 60bpm on the metronome and working up. Then the CAGED system shapes up and down the neck in one key, usually rotating which key each week.

### Scales

Minor pentatonic in five positions is the current focus, box 1 and box 2 are solid, boxes 3-5 still need conscious thought rather than being automatic. Practising at 100bpm, eighth notes, aiming to eventually push to 140bpm without the accuracy falling apart.

## Songs learned

A short list, not exhaustive, but the ones that stuck:

- **Ain't No Sunshine** — the fingerpicking pattern took about two weeks to get smooth, mostly a thumb independence issue.
- **Wonderwall** — obligatory, learned it in an evening, still play it when someone hands me a guitar at a party.
- **Layla (unplugged intro)** — this one is still a work in progress, the fingerpicked arpeggios in the intro are genuinely difficult to keep even.

## Song in progress: Little Wing intro

Started working on this in February 2024. The chord voicings themselves (Em, G, Am, that descending bass line) aren't hard on their own, but doing it with the thumb-over grip Hendrix used, while adding the little hammer-ons and pull-offs between chord changes, is a different level of coordination entirely. Current approach: isolate each two-bar phrase, loop it at 70% speed on a backing track app, and only move to the next phrase once it's clean three times in a row without stopping.

Roughly six weeks in and the first half of the intro is close to performance-ready. The second half, with the faster descending run, is still sloppy — timing drifts and a couple of the pull-offs don't ring out cleanly. Plan is to keep isolating that section rather than running the whole intro top to bottom, since running it end to end just reinforces the mistake at the point where it currently breaks down.

## Maintenance notes

Restrung and cleaned the Telecaster fretboard with a small amount of lemon oil in March 2024 — first proper clean in nearly two years, the rosewood had gone visibly dry and pale in patches. Also replaced the input jack on the Blues Junior after a crackling connection turned out to be a loose solder joint, fixed with a five-minute reflow rather than a full replacement part.
