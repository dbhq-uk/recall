# Allotment — seed and tuber orders

Ordering log for Plot 14b. Purely a record of what was bought, from whom, and for
how much. How each variety actually performed is in the plot notes, not here — I
keep these separate deliberately, because mixing "what I bought" with "what I
thought of it" is how the ordering list ends up as an essay every January and
nothing gets ordered on time.

Orders go in during the first week of January, seed potatoes arrive for chitting
in late February, everything else as and when.

## Suppliers

- **Thompson & Morgan** — seed potatoes only. Reliable, arrive well-packed and
  properly dormant rather than already sprouting in the bag.
- **Kings Seeds** — the cheap workhorse for anything sown by the packet. Their
  germination rates have never given me a reason to look elsewhere.
- **Chiltern Seeds** — Sungold, because nobody else seems to stock it reliably.
- **Suttons** — Crimson Crush. More expensive per packet than it feels like it
  should be, and ordered anyway, every year, without complaint.

## Standing note

Do not order more tomato seed. There are four unopened packets in the shed tin
going back to 2022 and tomato seed keeps for years. Every January I forget this
and buy more.
