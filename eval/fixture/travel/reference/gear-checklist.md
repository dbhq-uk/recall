# Gear checklist

Kit that's earned its place in the van after a few years of trips. Anything that got bought and then never used again isn't on this list any more.

## Kitchen

Cadac 2 Cobb stove as the main cooking setup — two gas burners plus the option to swap one ring out for the actual "Cobb" charcoal unit for evenings when we want a barbecue instead of a hob. Runs off a 6kg refillable propane bottle strapped into a vented locker behind the sliding door, well away from the leisure battery compartment.

Origo alcohol-fuel stove kept as a backup, single burner, no gas involved at all — bought after a gas regulator fault left us without cooking for an evening near Ullapool in 2022, cheap insurance against that happening again, and useful for a quick coffee without unstrapping the main stove for something small.

## Bedding

5cm memory foam mattress topper cut to fit over the fixed rock-and-roll style base, made a bigger difference to sleep quality than anything else bought for the van — the base cushions alone were noticeably firmer than either of us wanted for more than a couple of nights running.

## Awning

Vango Faro air awning, inflatable poles rather than traditional metal ones — pitches in about ten minutes solo, which matters when it's raining and you just want shelter up quickly. Rated to a fairly modest wind loading so it comes down if anything more than a stiff breeze is forecast overnight; not something to leave up unattended.

## Tools and recovery

- NOCO Boost Plus GB40 jump starter, 1000A peak — used exactly once so far but that one time (flat battery after leaving the fridge running with the engine off for two days longer than planned) justified the cost outright.
- MaXtrax recovery tracks, carried on the tow-bar rack rather than inside — used on soft ground at a farm campsite in Pembrokeshire where the field had gone soft overnight after rain and the van was sinking on pull-away.
- Tow rope rated to 3.5 tonnes, kept in the tool roll along with a basic socket set, cable ties, gaffer tape and a multimeter (added to the kit after the alternator failure on the M6 — see the breakdown notes).

## Packing list

Kept as a checklist file that gets run through before any trip longer than a weekend, split by category so nothing gets forgotten in the rush to leave:

```yaml
# packing-list.yaml
# reviewed before every trip over 2 nights

kitchen:
  - cadac_2cobb_stove
  - gas_bottle_spare_full   # always carry the spare, not just the one in use
  - origo_alcohol_stove
  - cooking_pans_set
  - water_containers_20l    # for grey water, see water-system notes

sleeping:
  - memory_foam_topper
  - sleeping_bags_x2
  - pillows

outdoor:
  - vango_faro_awning
  - camping_chairs_x2
  - table_folding

recovery_and_tools:
  - maxtrax_tracks
  - tow_rope
  - tool_roll
  - noco_boost_plus_gb40
  - multimeter

documents:
  - passports          # only for continental trips
  - vehicle_insurance_green_card
  - breakdown_cover_card
  - ferry_booking_confirmation
```

Learned the hard way to keep the documents section at the very end of the list rather than the start — it's the section most likely to get skipped when packing starts to feel finished, and it's also the one where forgetting something actually stops the trip rather than just being inconvenient.
