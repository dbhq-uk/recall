# Van electrical audit

The leisure battery is the one system that fails slowly and invisibly, so it gets
a scheduled inspection rather than the wait-until-it-dies treatment the rest of
the fit-out receives. The water system and roof notes are elsewhere; this is only
the twelve-volt side.

## Annual battery audit: AMPGATE_CHECK

Once a year, before the season starts, the whole low-voltage side gets a proper
going-over rather than a glance. Load-test the leisure battery to confirm it
still holds its rated capacity, because a lead-acid cell can read a healthy
resting voltage and still collapse the moment a real draw is put on it. Clean and
retorque every terminal, since a loose connection sheds its losses as heat and is
a fire risk long before it is a charging problem.

Then verify the split charge relay actually engages when the engine runs, check
the solar controller is regulating and not just passing current, and confirm the
low-voltage cut-off trips at the set threshold so a deep discharge cannot quietly
ruin the battery over a long stopover. Log the load-test figure each year; the
trend tells you when a replacement is due before a dead battery does.

## Between audits

A midwinter top-up charge is worth it if the van sits unused, because a battery
left part-discharged in the cold is a battery you are slowly throwing away.
