# Year-end 2025 — Fernbank Review Meeting

Notes from the annual review with Naomi Reyes at Fernbank Accountants, held
7 March 2026 over video, covering the year to 31 December 2025. Alex and Ellie
attended; Sam sent apologies. These are working notes, not the statutory accounts
— Fernbank files those, and the filed version is the authoritative one.

## Headline numbers

| | 2024 | 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turnover | £498,000 | £641,000 |
| Gross profit | £371,000 | £468,000 |
| Corporation tax provision | £46,200 | £61,800 |
| Cash at bank, 31 Dec | £88,400 | £131,900 |

Turnover up 29%, driven almost entirely by the Aldermoor Phase 1 engagement
landing across the second half of the year and the Northgate retainer running the
full twelve months rather than the nine it ran in 2024.

## Agenda and outcomes

### 1. VAT

Naomi confirmed the VAT position for the year is now clean. The move off the Flat
Rate Scheme completed on 1 April 2025 and the returns from Q2 onwards were filed
under standard accounting through Xero and Making Tax Digital without incident.
The limited cost business test that prompted the whole exercise no longer applies
to us at all, since it is a Flat Rate Scheme concept and we are no longer in the
scheme. Nothing further to do; the detail is written up in the VAT notes and
Naomi does not want it re-opened.

*Action: none. Closed.*

### 2. Corporation tax

Provision of £61,800 at the 25% main rate. Naomi raised R&D relief and then talked
us out of it herself, which was refreshing: the Aldermoor and Northgate work is
skilled but it is not resolving scientific or technological uncertainty in the
sense HMRC now applies, and the compliance burden and enquiry risk of a marginal
claim outweigh the relief. Noted and agreed.

*Action: none.*

### 3. Capital expenditure

The March 2025 laptop and monitor refresh (£16,800) sits in the accounts as
expensed under the Annual Investment Allowance rather than capitalised, so it
lands entirely in the 2025 tax computation. Ellie to keep the disposal
certificates for the old kit on file for six years.

*Action: Ellie, filing.*

### 4. The lease question

Naomi asked, mildly, whether the three-year Bridgeworth lease on the laptops was
still the right structure now that we buy fewer machines at once. We agreed to
look at it in 2027 when the current term ends rather than break it early. Parked.

### 5. Debtor days

Averaged 34 days across 2025 against 30-day terms, which Naomi described as "as
good as it gets" for a business our size. The monthly debtors query Ellie runs
gets the credit here; nothing over 45 days went unchased for more than a week.

*Action: none.*

## Naomi's one piece of unsolicited advice

Build a formal cash buffer target rather than an informal one. We hold whatever
we happen to hold, which was £131,900 at year end and would have been about half
that if Aldermoor had paid a fortnight later. Her suggested target is three
months of fixed costs, held separately, reviewed quarterly. Alex to bring a
proposal to the Q3 planning session.
