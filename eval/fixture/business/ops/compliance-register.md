# Compliance Register

A single index of every policy, registration, certificate and determination we
are obliged to hold, where the evidence lives, and when it next needs attention.

This is an *index*. It records that a thing exists and where to find it. It does
not reproduce the contents — cover levels, contract terms, determination
reasoning and so on all live in the underlying documents, and duplicating them
here would create two versions of the truth, one of which would be wrong within a
year. Ellie reviews the whole register on the first Monday of each quarter.

current PDF of each in the shared drive rather than emailing Cavendish for a
fresh copy every time, which we did four times in 2025 before Ellie put a stop to
it.

insurance and suppliers note, not here.

## Gaps we know about

Honesty is more useful here than a tidy table:

- We have no formal policy threshold for when a short associate engagement is
  "too short to bother running CEST". This has been an acknowledged judgement
  call for over a year and it should be written down.
- We claim to work "in a manner aligned with ISO 27001" for one client. We are
  not certified and the alignment is not audited by anyone. That is defensible,
  but only for as long as we say it exactly that carefully.
